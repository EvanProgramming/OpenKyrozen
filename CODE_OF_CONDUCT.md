# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).

## Our pledge

We welcome participation in OpenKyrozen from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. We aim to make project spaces respectful, inclusive, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and empathy, including when you disagree.
- Give and receive constructive feedback in good faith.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work to repair harm.
- Focus discussion on the project and its technical merits.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, personal attacks, discriminatory or demeaning conduct, and sharing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when someone represents OpenKyrozen in public. Project maintainers may clarify these standards and remove or decline contributions that violate them.

Report conduct concerns privately to [evangonggyf@gmail.com](mailto:evangonggyf@gmail.com). Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with care for the reporter's privacy. Depending on the severity and pattern of behavior, maintainers may issue a private correction or warning, restrict contact or participation for a period, or permanently exclude someone from project spaces.
