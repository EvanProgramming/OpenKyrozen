## Review focus
- No schema/policy bypass via compatibility dictionary mutation or dynamic registration.
- No shared bound executor across workspaces/users; child callers cannot gain orchestration tools.
- Schema copies/snapshots cannot mutate authoritative metadata.
- Dynamic tools retain restrictive capability and existing source-validation/approval/audit gates.
- Capabilities do not imply side-effect freedom or parallel safety; browser, graph refresh and orchestration remain nonparallel.
