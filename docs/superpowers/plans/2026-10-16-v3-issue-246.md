## Goal and boundaries

Allow Working Plan replanning only within the currently accepted Strategic Plan. Investigation, verification, ordering, dependencies, and task decomposition that preserve approved objectives, acceptance criteria, workspace, effects, capabilities, and risk may proceed automatically. Objective/acceptance changes, expanded authority or scope, increased risk, and unprovable effects require a new user-approved Strategic Plan version.

Keep the accepted version authoritative while a material proposal is pending. Do not dispatch further mutations under an unresolved proposal. Approval authorizes only the revised strategic bounds; it does not grant capabilities, approve tools, or replay prior work. Preserve existing tool approval and evidence gates.
