## Scope and ownership

Extend the current `TaskManager`, typed `update_plan` registry tool, `TasksRepository`, and existing SQLite schema. The repository owns atomic persistence and optimistic revision checks; `TaskManager` validates a defensive candidate and updates its in-memory projection only after commit. Preserve `complexity`/`tasks` callers through the same validation and transaction path. Do not add revision-history storage (#244), Strategic Plan behavior (#245), or material-change approval (#246).
